New Staff Technology Setup

Complete these steps in order. Setup pages will open in separate windows so you can return here after each task.

Important: Do not sign into your assigned district device with the temporary password. Change your password first, complete account security setup, then sign into your district device with your new permanent password.
Setup progress 0 of 5 complete

Account setup complete

Please see System Support. You are ready to receive your district device and complete your first device sign-in.

1

Change Your Temporary Password

Open InfoSystem and establish your permanent district password before using your assigned device.

Not started
  1. Open InfoSystem using the button below.
  2. Sign in with the username and temporary password provided by System Support.
  3. Follow the prompts to create your permanent password.
  4. Do not use the temporary password on your district device.
Return here after your permanent password has been created.
2

Set Up InfoSystem 2FA

InfoSystem 2FA is required within 3 days and uses Google Authenticator.

Locked
  1. Install Google Authenticator on your phone if you do not already have it.
  2. Open the InfoSystem 2FA setup/instructions.
  3. Scan the QR code provided by InfoSystem.
  4. Enter the verification code to confirm enrollment.
Confirm that InfoSystem accepts your authenticator code.
3

Verify Your Google Account

Confirm that your district Google account accepts your new permanent password.

Locked
  1. Open the Google sign-in page.
  2. Sign in with your district email address.
  3. Use the permanent password you created in Step 1.
  4. Confirm that you can access your district Google account.
Return here after you have successfully signed into Google.
4

Set Up Google 2-Step Verification

Google 2-Step Verification must be configured within 2 weeks of account creation.

Locked
  1. Open Google 2-Step Verification.
  2. Choose an approved verification method.
  3. Complete enrollment and verify that it works.
  4. If your enrollment deadline has already passed, ask System Support for assistance.
Make sure Google shows that 2-Step Verification is configured.
5

Sign Out of This Setup Station

Protect your account before the next employee uses this device.

Locked
  1. Sign out of InfoSystem.
  2. Sign out of your Google account on this device.
  3. Close any setup windows that are still open.
  4. Return here and mark this step complete.
Do not leave your Google or InfoSystem session signed in on this kiosk.
